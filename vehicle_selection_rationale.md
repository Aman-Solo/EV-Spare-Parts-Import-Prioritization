# Vehicle selection rationale

**Question:** which EV spare parts should be prioritised for import into Ethiopia? The study covers three vehicles. Each one was chosen because it is visible in the Ethiopian market and because its parts can be traced to a supplier. All counts below are **marketplace listing counts** from the Adey Market category pages, collected 2026-11-16 (one page inspected per category). A listing count shows how visible a model is in the market. **It is not registrations, sales or a count of distinct cars**, because one car can be listed by several sellers or re-posted.

| Vehicle | Adey listing count (sellers) | Platform-stated typical price | Other Ethiopian evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| BYD Yuan Up (Atto 2) | **1,362 listings** (283 sellers), 57 pages | 5,400,000 ETB (platform figure) | Gezat Electric catalogue page; BYD Ethiopia specification page |
| BYD Seagull (Dolphin Mini / Atto 1) | **764 listings** (177 sellers), 32 pages | 3,800,000 ETB (platform figure) | Gezat Electric catalogue page; BYD Ethiopia specification page |
| Volkswagen ID.4 | **102 listings** (59 sellers), 5 pages | 5,700,000 ETB (platform figure) | Individual Adey and Gezat listings (e.g. a used 2022 ID.4 in Addis Ababa) |

A re-check on 2026-11-16 (Stage 4) showed small day-to-day drift: Yuan Up 1,361 / 284, Seagull 759 / 180, ID.4 105 / 60. The dataset keeps the first observation, and the re-check is recorded as a proposal in `verification_stage4.md`.

## Why each vehicle matters

**BYD Yuan Up.** This is the most-listed of the three on Adey (1,362 listings from 283 sellers), so it is the most visible small electric SUV in the inspected market. BYD Ethiopia publishes a local specification page (front vented disc and rear disc brakes, MacPherson front suspension), which means the brand has an official local presence. Its parts evidence comes from a model-specific catalogue (Connect Drive, Albania) with original BYD numbers for 7 of 10 categories.

**BYD Seagull.** This is the second most-listed (764 listings, 177 sellers) and the lowest typical price of the three. That points to a mass-market city car, the kind of vehicle that generates routine consumable and collision-repair demand. It has the strongest part-number evidence in the study: 9 of 10 categories have a specific record, and in 5 of them two independent Chinese catalogues (Landin Parts, CQS) list the same OE number.

**Volkswagen ID.4.** This is a much smaller visible fleet (102 listings), but it is the only one of the three sold in the US. That gives it public failure evidence (NHTSA: 1,040 unique complaints and 26 recall campaigns for MY2021-2026), and its parts are covered by large European and US aftermarket catalogues. It acts as a **contrast case**: a European-brand EV, imported mostly as used vehicles, with better international documentation but a smaller local fleet.

## Why not others

* **Toyota bZ4X** had grouped bZ-series counts (1,021 for the whole family) that cannot be attributed to the bZ4X alone. Individual listings were expired or blocked.
* **Changan BenBen E-Star** (279 Adey listings) had weaker international parts traceability in the earlier batches.

## What this rationale does not claim

* It does not claim how many of these cars are registered or on the road in Ethiopia (no registration data was available).
* It does not claim that these are the best-selling EVs in Ethiopia. Listing counts reflect what sellers post, and Telegram and Facebook sellers outside Adey's index were not counted.
